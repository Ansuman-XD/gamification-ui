Rules of Engagement

Before you begin, please adhere to the following rules of engagement:

1. Honesty First

Complete this work independently. Do not use outside help or unauthorized resources.

2. No Distractions

Ensure your environment is clear of prohibited materials and that you remain focused on the task.

3. Fairness

Respect the integrity of this assessment and your peers by following all instructions strictly.

4. Accountability

Any breach of exam ethics will be reported and handled according to the assessment policy.
